Weather 14-26-Ceircle
Android package: app.weather.usa.forecast.g26galaxy

Choose your weather location

Open the side menu and manage your locations to search for a city. You can use a saved city without granting device location permission. Allow location access only if you want weather for your current position.

Weather is not updating

Check your connection, confirm the selected city and try refreshing. Cached information can be older than current conditions. If the provider is temporarily unavailable, try again later. Include the city and time of the problem when contacting support; never send provider keys or account passwords.

Widgets and notifications

The Widgets page contains seven weather widgets. Select a preview and follow your launcher's widget prompt, or add a widget from your home-screen widget picker. Widget support depends on the launcher. Notifications can be changed in the app and in Android Settings. Battery and background restrictions can delay refreshes.

Paid ad removal and available offers

If paid ad removal is available in your build and region, open the app's purchase screen to view the offers returned by Samsung Galaxy Store. An offer may be a one-time purchase or an auto-renewing subscription. Check the store's current price, billing period, renewal terms and any trial conditions before confirming. Product availability and pricing come from the store; an unavailable offer cannot be purchased through the app.

Restore a Samsung purchase

Sign in to Galaxy Store with the same Samsung account that made the purchase, connect to the internet and choose Restore purchases in the app's purchase screen. This checks eligible ownership for this app; it does not make a new payment. A canceled subscription may still be available until its current entitlement period ends. Purchases from a different app or store are not automatically transferred.

If paid status does not appear, check the Samsung account, Galaxy Store connection and whether the purchase or subscription is still valid. Retry restoration before attempting another purchase. Contact ahmetefeerdal3@gmail.com with the app version and a description; if a receipt is needed, hide unrelated personal information. Never send your account password or full payment-card details. Test/sandbox purchases are separate from confirmed paid production purchases.

Manage a subscription

If you purchased an auto-renewing offer, open Galaxy Store and use its Subscriptions section to review or cancel it. Uninstalling this app or clearing its data does not cancel the subscription. Review the renewal terms and current status in Galaxy Store. Contact Samsung or use the store's help options for checkout and refund questions.

Updates and maintenance

Update notices open the app's store listing. If the app displays maintenance information, use Retry or return later. A force-update notice requires a supported store version before normal use can continue. The app itself does not download or install APKs.
